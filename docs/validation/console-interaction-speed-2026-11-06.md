# Console interaction speed — offline validation, November 6, 2026

The normal cached-credential reviewed command route previously used 14 `vmrun` child processes: five in preparation and nine in execution. These are code-path counts, excluding top-level Python processes, controller handoff and review. Preparation and execution each capture a screenshot and open a VNC connection. Five retained historical execution receipts took 18.38–19.66 seconds (median 19.33 seconds); this excludes preparation, review and handoff and does not isolate individual calls.

Execution now reuses one authenticated, VM-identity-pinned guest session for its two full game PID/start/SHA queries. The normal route consequently has 13 rather than 14 `vmrun` children (execution 8 instead of 9); both identity queries, fresh review, exclusive mutex, capture and release remain. A 20-trial simulated call-delay model excluding the initial `vmrun list`, with 10 ms per call, produced median 82.41 ms before and 72.26 ms after (8 versus 7 guest calls); it measures the assumed delay model, not actual guest performance. No live timing was measured. The 5–15 second preparation and execution targets remain goals, not results.
